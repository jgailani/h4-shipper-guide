What can I ship?

Know before you plan

Most food and packaged goods are welcome. A few things are not.

✓

Ordinary food cargo

Fresh produce, seafood, meat, and dry or packaged goods are generally accepted, subject to inspection.

✕

Not accepted

Electronics are not accepted under the initial service.

!

Restricted

Dangerous goods, such as aerosols and lithium batteries, need special approval. Dry ice must be declared in advance.
